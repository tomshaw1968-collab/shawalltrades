What we do

Two trades, one contractor.

Most jobs touch both building and roofing work — having one contractor cover both means fewer people to coordinate and no gaps between trades.

SVC.01 — ROOFING

Roofing & repairs

Storm damage repairs, tile and slate replacement, full re-roofs, flat roof installations, and chimney stack repairs. Experience across clay, concrete and natural slate.

SVC.02 — BUILDING

Building projects

Domestic building work and development projects, from extensions and structural alterations to full renovation jobs, start to finish.

SVC.03 — JOINERY

Joinery installation

SVC.04 — EXTERIOR

Fascia, soffit & guttering

About

A Stepps-based crew, six years in.

Shaw All Trades Limited has been trading since January 2020, based in Stepps on the north-east edge of Glasgow. The work covers general building, roofing and joinery for homeowners across the city and the surrounding towns.

Every job starts with a proper look at the problem before any quote goes out — no guesswork on a roof you can't see the state of from the ground. See recent jobs on the projects page.
